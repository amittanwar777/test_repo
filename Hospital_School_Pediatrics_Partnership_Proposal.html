Program overview

Tie-up with nearby schools

The hospital proposes to partner directly with nearby schools, embedding pediatric care into the school year rather than waiting for families to come to the hospital.

Each enrolled student receives a structured, ongoing health partnership — regular checkups, easy access to consultation, and a single record that follows them as they grow.

It is a low-friction way for schools to offer real healthcare value to parents, and a natural, trust-building front door for the hospital into the community it serves.

Getting started

Implementation roadmap

Phase 1
Sign a hospital–school MoU formalizing the partnership terms.
Phase 2
Collect student enrollment and parental consent.
Phase 3
Issue an annual health card to every enrolled student.
Phase 4
Conduct biannual health camps on school premises.
Phase 5
Route referrals and follow-up care back to the hospital.
